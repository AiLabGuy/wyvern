# Assets

## Wyvern Browser app icon (assets/icon/*)

Licensed CC-BY-4.0, except that no trademark rights are granted (see TRADEMARKS.md).
Provenance: Initial concept generated with Grok Imagine (SpaceXAI) from text prompts and
a prior Grok-generated reference; Created with Grok. The shipped SVG was traced
(potrace, seven flat tones) and cleaned up from the Grok output by a human contributor,
who also recolored the eyes and tongue and made the simplified small-size variant.
AI-generated elements may not be eligible for copyright protection; copyright is claimed
only in the human edits.

Files: wyvern-icon.svg (master), wyvern-icon-small.svg (16–32 px master) and PNG renders
at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 px. The full license text is in LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt.

`scripts/make-icon-variants.py` derives three 512 px alternates from `wyvern-icon-1024.png`
(Graphite, Hermes, Neon) into `Resources/IconVariants/`. The master files in this folder
are not modified. Each alternate has a `.license` sidecar with the same CC-BY-4.0 terms.
